La Jolla Freedive Club · Journey Map

Lead → Qualified Candidate → Reserved Seat → Ready Student

The objective is not maximum lead volume. The objective is operational relief: cleanly handle the students already arriving, make the deposit the seat-reservation boundary, and automate onboarding until each student is ready for course day.

01

Inquiry received

Candidate chooses a course/date or asks for help. Capture course, experience, preferred dates, group size, contact.

02

Needs qualification

Check age, swim ability, prerequisite, medical risk, gear, and course fit. Do not overburden the form.

03

Date proposed

Offer open course options or group-rate opportunities. AI draft replies can support Joshua here.

04

Deposit requested

Candidate gets payment link. Seat is explicitly not reserved yet.

05

Seat reserved

Stripe confirms deposit/full payment. Booking confirmed. Inquiry linked. Magic link issued.

06

Onboarding incomplete

Portal shows remaining medical, AIDA, waiver, gear, and prep requirements.

07

Ready for course

Readiness score is complete. Student gets logistics. Joshua gets briefing.

08

Course completed

Follow-up, certification path, Saturday Sessions invite, review/referral.

First implementation bias

  • Make the funnel state model explicit before adding more automation.
  • Use existing routes and tables; avoid building a full CRM.
  • Move all post-payment next steps into the portal.
  • Make `/admin/inquiries` the pre-payment cockpit.
  • Keep the inquiry form lightweight but qualifying.

Core rule

A student is not holding a course seat until a deposit or full payment is received.
